## Size after encoding
3,178,679 rows × 210 features (197 one-hot levels of fiscal_year, quarter, visa_class, case_status, full_time, unit_of_pay, worksite_state, worksite_region, employer_state, soc_group + 13 numeric/flags) = **667,522,590** cells (floor 3,000,000: PASS). SOC titles are encoded as soc_group: the 50 most common (87.7% of rows) + 'Other'; the full 1,123-title version would give ~1,280 features, almost all for titles with a handful of rows. employer_key and job_title are not one-hot encoded (132k / 400k+ levels, almost all tiny); they enter through employer_volume, n_identical and the seniority flags.
